How this works — read this first (2 minutes)

  • Time: about 75 minutes for the graded core, plus about 20 minutes if you do the Module 4 extension. Core total: 100 points. The extension is scored separately out of 25, and clearly-marked bonus items (up to +4) can never hurt your grade.
  • Learn while you work: almost every item asks you to commit to a prediction before anything is revealed. Guessing after you see the answer teaches nothing — predicting first is where the learning happens. Your first prediction is kept in your results, so you can see what your instincts got right.
  • Feedback explains why, not just right/wrong. If you miss, read the explanation — it names the exact lesson to revisit.
  • Second tries: on questions with 4+ choices and on typed / built / written answers, a correct second try earns half credit. On 2–3 choice questions there is no second-try credit (elimination would make it free). Multi-part items earn credit per correct part.
  • Hints are free. Hint 1 is a concept reminder; Hint 2 is a bigger nudge. Using hints is recorded in your results (so your instructor can see where the class got stuck), but it never lowers your score — being stuck and using help is part of learning.
  • Simulated vs. real: the little phone previews in the extension are simulations with fixed, known outcomes that match the course’s SwiftUI preview for exactly the cases shown. The written-Swift checks test the language rules taught in the lessons, not a full compiler.
  • Your work saves automatically in this browser. At the end, use Download results or Print to turn it in, exactly as your instructor directs.
  • You will actually write Swift in three places. Spelling of keywords matters; spacing doesn't. Equivalent correct Swift is accepted.
